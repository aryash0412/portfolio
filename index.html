About Me

Hello! I'm Aryash Yelkar, a passionate and detail-oriented 3D Artist with a strong focus on product modeling, props creation, and the development of high-quality 3D assets. With a keen eye for realism and precision, I specialize in crafting visually compelling models that are both technically sound and aesthetically engaging.

My work spans across product visualization, gaming assets, and digital media, where I strive to bring concepts to life through meticulous attention to detail. I emphasize clean topology, efficient geometry, and optimized workflows to ensure that every asset is production-ready. I work with industry-standard software such as Maya, Blender, Substance Painter, ZBrush, After Effects, and Photoshop as part of my workflow, allowing me to handle everything from modeling and sculpting to texturing, rendering, and post-production.

In addition, I pay close attention to material accuracy, texturing, and realistic lighting setups, enabling me to create renders that closely mimic real-world visuals. I enjoy the full creative pipeline, integrating multiple tools to achieve polished and professional results.

I am deeply passionate about transforming ideas into immersive 3D experiences. Whether it's a sleek product design or an intricate prop, I enjoy the entire creative process—from concept development to final render. I am also committed to continuous learning, regularly exploring new tools, techniques, and industry trends to refine my craft and stay ahead in the evolving world of 3D art.

Driven by creativity and precision, I aim to deliver high-quality assets that not only meet technical standards but also captivate and inspire.
